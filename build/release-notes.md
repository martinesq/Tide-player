## Tide 4.7.6

### Added
- SoundCloud source selector (YT Music / SoundCloud / YT & SC) with sign-in, added to the sidebar and sign-in screen.
- Source badges on thumbnails and the now-playing bar when using YT & SC.
- Pick any font installed on your system, from Settings > Appearance.
- Custom button text color, from Settings > Appearance.
- Likes layout: show your Likes as a grid of covers or a numbered list with durations.
- Now-playing titles that are too long to fit now scroll in the player bar and fullscreen player.

Tide is an independent fork of [Orchard](https://github.com/sfg5453/orchard) by SFG545.
